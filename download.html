Installing it

  1. Download the APK. Tap the button above on your Android device, or move the file across from a desktop.
  2. Allow the install. Android will ask permission to install from your browser or file manager. Grant it for that app, then confirm.
  3. Open Akalynth. You wake in Rookguard. Answer the guardian, and the gate to High City opens.

Installing from a direct APK is normal on Android — the permission prompt is the operating system asking whether it should trust the app you downloaded from, not a warning about Akalynth.
